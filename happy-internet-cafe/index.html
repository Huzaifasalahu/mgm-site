About Us

Welcome to Happy Internet Café, your trusted partner for all digital services at Federal University Gashua, Nigeria. We are committed to providing fast, reliable, and affordable ICT solutions to meet the needs of students, staff, and the wider community.

Our modern facility is equipped with high-speed internet connectivity, state-of-the-art computers, and professional equipment to ensure you get the best service every time.

Our Mission

To provide affordable and reliable digital services that empower students and the community with access to essential ICT resources.

Our Vision

To become the leading campus ICT service center, recognized for excellence, innovation, and customer satisfaction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

What are your operating hours?

We operate from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, Monday through Saturday. On Sundays, we're open from 12:00 PM to 6:00 PM.

Do you offer bulk printing discounts?

Yes! We offer special discounts for bulk printing orders. Contact us directly for custom pricing on large projects.

Can I save my files on your computers?

We recommend using your personal USB drive or cloud storage. However, we can assist with temporary file transfers via email.

Do you provide home delivery for services?

Currently, our services are available at our physical location. However, we're exploring delivery options for the future.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash payments and bank transfers. Mobile money options are also available for your convenience.
